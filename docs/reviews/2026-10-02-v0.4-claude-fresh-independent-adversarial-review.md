## Blocking structural defects

### S1 — v0.4 does not define its relationship to v0.3

v0.3 is substantially larger and contains contracts omitted by v0.4. v0.4 does not say whether it replaces v0.3 or acts as a delta. As a replacement it drops important contracts; as a delta it does not specify which clauses it replaces.

### S2 — N01–N08 cannot be checked durably

The repository does not contain a v0.3 review artifact defining N01–N08, and v0.4 has no closure matrix mapping those findings to clauses.

### S3 — C23–C34 are undefined

v0.3 defines only C01–C22. v0.4 says C01–C34 are inherited but does not define C23–C34, so the requested cases have no durable expected behavior.

### S4 — Concepts are listed without decision rules

Status values, budget categories, evidence claim types, Support Slice fields, Recipe parts and Gate protocol fields are named but do not have sufficient transition, combination or decision rules.

## Status attack cases

### Case A — requested first three pages, pages 1–3 all succeed

Coverage may be interpreted as `VERIFIED_COMPLETE` against the requested scope or `VERIFIED_SUBSET` against the parent collection. The contract does not force one answer.

### Case B — whole collection requested, budget runs out before page 3 discovery

A reasonable result may be request `PARTIAL/UNKNOWN`, selected acquisition `COMPLETE`, and coverage `TRUNCATED/UNKNOWN`, but the document lacks a `StopReason` and legal combination matrix. Conflicting combinations are not excluded.

### Case C — collection has no accessible resources

`SelectionAcquisitionStatus` has `EMPTY`, but Coverage has no explicit empty state. The contract does not prevent an empty result from being interpreted as complete.

## Minimum blocking conclusions

Three defects independently block Product Freeze:

1. C23–C34 have no durable definitions.
2. There are no legal combination rules for the status model.
3. Gate protocols have fields but no sufficient executable decision rules.

The reviewer concluded that v0.4 contains the right concepts but does not yet provide a uniquely executable product contract.
